## Why previous checks remained incomplete

- Long child reports were truncated during collection; coverage was not reconciled before stopping.
- Retry selection repeatedly picked the alphabetically first inaccessible conferences, starving later entries. Selection now prioritizes never-attempted/oldest-attempted entries.
- Comparing differently shaped masterRow dictionaries erased earlier evidence. The unsafe helpers were repaired; lost judgments were marked for re-verification, not silently counted as confirmed.
- Missing timezone/extension text was incorrectly used as a publication blocker. A confirmed official edition, track and date are sufficient; unspecified details remain unspecified.
- Confirmed entries were not necessarily freshly re-read after a later extension (as ENASE demonstrated). A verification timestamp is a snapshot, not permanent validation.
